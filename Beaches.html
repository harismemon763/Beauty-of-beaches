NORTH BEACHES

Yyteri Beach

Location: Pori, Finland (Nordic region)

One of the longest and most popular natural sand beaches in the Nordic countries, famous for its dunes and windsurfing.

Rauðisandur Beach

Location: Westfjords, Iceland (North Atlantic)

A striking beach known for its rare red-and-golden sand stretching beneath massive, dramatic cliffs.

Skeleton Coast Beaches

Location: Namibia (Northern coastline / Atlantic)

While harsh and rugged, it features mesmerizing desert-meets-ocean landscapes dotted with shipwrecks.

Schiermonnikoog Beaches

Location: Schiermonnikoog Island, Netherlands (Northern Europe)

Pristine, wide stretches of white sand located in a national park with some of the cleanest air and widest shores in Europe.

Lawrencetown Beach

Location: Nova Scotia, Canada (North America / East Coast, but northern latitude)

A world-famous cold-water surf destination surrounded by rugged Canadian headlands.

EAST BEACHES

Anse Source d'Argent

Location: La Digue Island, Seychelles (Indian Ocean / East of Africa)

Widely considered one of the most photographed beaches on earth, famous for its iconic pinkish sand and giant granite boulders.

El Nido Beach

Location: Palawan, Philippines (Western Pacific)

Known for its clear turquoise waters, limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and beautiful island scenery.

Boracay Beach

Location: Boracay Island, Philippines (Western Pacific)

Famous for its soft white sand, clear blue water, and relaxing coastal scenery.

Maya Bay

Location: Phi Phi Islands, Thailand (Andaman Sea)

A beautiful tropical bay surrounded by limestone cliffs, known for its clear water and dramatic scenery.

Radhanagar Beach

Location: Havelock Island, India (Bay of Bengal)

A scenic tropical beach known for its white sand, calm blue water, and spectacular sunsets.

SOUTH BEACHES

Bazaruto Beach

Location: Bazaruto Island, Mozambique (Indian Ocean)

A beautiful tropical beach known for its white sand, clear water, and peaceful island scenery.

Boulders Beach

Location: Cape Town, South Africa (Atlantic Ocean)

A scenic beach known for its sheltered coastline, granite boulders, and famous African penguin colony.

Nungwi Beach

Location: Zanzibar, Tanzania (Indian Ocean)

A tropical beach with white sand and turquoise water, popular for its beautiful sunsets and coastal views.

Whitehaven Beach

Location: Whitsunday Island, Australia (Coral Sea)

Famous for its brilliant white silica sand and clear turquoise water stretching along the Whitsunday coast.

Clifton Beach

Location: Cape Town, South Africa (Atlantic Ocean)

A popular coastal beach surrounded by large granite boulders and dramatic mountain scenery.

WEST BEACHES

Playa del Amor

Location: Marietas Islands, Mexico (Pacific Ocean)

A unique hidden beach surrounded by rock formations, known for its natural cave-like setting and clear water.

Santa Monica Beach

Location: California, USA (Pacific Ocean)

A famous urban beach known for its wide sandy coastline, ocean views, and iconic pier.

Horseshoe Bay Beach

Location: Southampton Parish, Bermuda (Atlantic Ocean)

A beautiful curved beach known for its soft pinkish sand, clear water, and rocky coastal landscape.

Copacabana Beach

Location: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (Atlantic Ocean)

One of Brazil's most famous beaches, known for its long shoreline, lively atmosphere, and beautiful coastal views.

Doctor's Cave Beach

Location: Montego Bay, Jamaica (Caribbean Sea)

A well-known Caribbean beach famous for its clear turquoise water, soft sand, and calm swimming conditions.
